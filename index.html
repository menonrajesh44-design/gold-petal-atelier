BEAUTY, THOUGHTFULLY KEPT

A little care
goes a long way.

Where should I display my flowers?

Keep preserved and dried flowers indoors, away from direct sunlight, water, steam, excess humidity and heat sources.

How do I handle and clean them?

Lift an arrangement by its container or base. Avoid pressing petals or bending dried stems. Use a soft, dry brush or gentle cool air for loose surface dust. For enclosed frames, dust the glass only.

Can I choose a colour or size?

Share the design reference, preferred palette, display location and desired size. Gold Petal can confirm the materials, available variations, packaging and final specification for your selection.

How do I check price and availability?

Send an enquiry with the reference and quantity. Price, lead time, flower treatment and accessories are confirmed for each selection; the catalogue does not provide live stock or prices.
